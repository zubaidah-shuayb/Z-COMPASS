# Z-COMPASS Roadmap

Z-COMPASS is an intelligent reflection and decision-support experience designed to help people slow down, understand what's really happening, and find a clearer direction.

This roadmap documents the journey from the first concept to the current contest-ready product.

## 🌱 The Journey Continues

Z-COMPASS is not designed to tell people what to do.

It's designed to help them pause, look at their situation from different angles, understand what matters most, and choose their own direction.

**You bring the mess. Z-COMPASS helps you find the direction inside it.**
